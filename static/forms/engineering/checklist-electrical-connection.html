CHECKLIST FOR ELECTRICAL CONNECTION

Requirements for DORELCO Power Connection & Meter Clearance
  • Approved Application Form from DORELCO: Official pre-approved power utility connection application form from Don Orestes Romualdez Electric Cooperative, Inc.
  • Electrical Layout Plan: Complete wiring and power layout diagrams signed and sealed by a Professional Electrical Engineer (PEE).
  • Duly Accomplished Electrical Permit Form: Official municipal electrical permit application form completely filled out and signed.
  • Tax Declaration R13 (2 Photocopies): Current updated municipal property tax declaration certificate.
  • Mode of Ownership (2 Photocopies): Proof of ownership or legal right over the property (Transfer Certificate of Title, Deed of Absolute Sale, or Notarized Lease Agreement/Consent).
  • Pabahay Beneficiary Documents (if applicable): 2 Photocopies of Entry Pass and Certificate of Award for verified government housing/resettlement beneficiaries.
  • Photographs of the House/Structure: Clear photos showing the structure inside and outside with the Agricultural & Biosystems Engineer / supervising engineer (ABE), duly signed by the ABE.
  • Detailed Engineering Drawings (5 Sets): Complete structural and architectural drawings signed and sealed by a licensed Civil Engineer or Architect.
  • Barangay Certification / Clearance (1 Photocopy): Valid clearance certificate issued by the host barangay where the structure is situated.
  • Locational Clearance: Zoning and land use locational clearance issued by the Municipal Planning & Development Coordinator (MPDC).
  • Reduced Copy of Electrical Layout Plan (1 Copy): Standard handy scale copy for official field inspection and utility meter verification.
  • Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC): Clear fire safety certification issued by the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) Tanauan Station.
