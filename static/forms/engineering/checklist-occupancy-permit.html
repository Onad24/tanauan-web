OFFICE OF THE BUILDING OFFICIAL

CHECKLIST FOR OCCUPANCY PERMIT

Pursuant to Section 309 of the National Building Code of the Philippines (P.D. 1096)
  • 5 Copies of duly accomplished & notarized Certificate of Completion: Signed by the owner/applicant and signed & sealed by the licensed Architect/Civil Engineer in-charge of the construction, together with approved plans and specifications. If the construction was undertaken through a contract, the Certificate of Completion shall be signed by the PCAB Contractor/AMO.
  • 5 Copies of duly accomplished Unified Application Form for Occupancy: Signed by the owner/applicant and signed & sealed by the licensed Architect/Civil Engineer in-charge of the construction.
  • 1 copy of the Construction Logbook: Complete with records of the construction processes, dates, tests, inspections, and daily progress signed by the supervising engineer.
  • 3 Photocopies of valid PRC Licenses & PTR: Current Professional Regulation Commission IDs and Professional Tax Receipts of all involved design and supervising professionals.
  • Photographs of the Structure: Showing substantial completion of the building exterior, including clear views of the Front, Sides, and Rear areas.
  • 2 Photocopies of Approved Building Permit Certificate: Official copy issued by the Tanauan Office of the Building Official.
  • 2 Photocopies of Order of Payment: Official receipt and assessment from the Building Permit application stage.
  • 4 Sets of As-Built Plans (If changes occurred): In cases where there are changes/modifications in the building plans covered by the issued Building Permit, 4 sets of As-built plans are required to be submitted to the OBO reflecting all amendments, subject to NBCP, Referral Codes, Zoning, and Fire Code.
  • Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC): Official clearance issued by the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) / Municipal Fire Station.
