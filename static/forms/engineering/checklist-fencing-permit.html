LIST OF DOCUMENTS REQUIRED IN ORDER LACKING REMARKS
1) Fencing Permit Application Form (7 copies, signed and sealed by licensed professional)
2) Construction Logbook (2 official copies)
Plans & Specifications:
a) Perspective & Location Plan (Page 1)
b) Site Development Plan (Scale 1:200m)
c) Floor Plan (Scale 1:100m)
d) Elevations (Front, Rear, Sides, Scale 1:100m, Page 2)
e) At least 2 Sections (Scale 1:100m)
f) Foundation Plan (Scale 1:100m)
g) Structural Details: Columns, Wall & Column Footings (Scale 1:20m)
3) Bill of Materials & Cost Estimate (Typed, 7 copies, signed & sealed)
4) Building Specifications (Original copy, 7 copies)
5) Clearances Affecting Use of Building / Land:
a) Zoning / Land Use Clearance from MPDC
b) Locational Clearance from MPDC (5 copies)
c) Lot Technical Description from DENR (5 copies)
d) Anti-Pollution Clearance from DENR (for commercial/industrial properties)
6) Proof of Land Ownership / Right to Use: (5 copies each)
a) Current Real Property Tax Receipt
b) Tax Declaration / Certificate of Title (Authenticated)
c) Certificate of Title from Registry of Deeds / Deed of Absolute Sale / Lease Contract
7) Fire Safety Clearance Certificate from BFP (if electrical layout is included in fence design)
8) Professional Credentials: PTR & PRC IDs of Civil Engineer & Architect who signed/sealed plans (5 copies)
9) Supplies: 1 pc. Expanding Envelope + Folder & Paper Fastener
10) Barangay Clearance: a) As to personality, b) As to line and grade (RROW)
11) DPWH Road Right of Way Clearance (If structure is along National Highway)
📌 Important Reminders:
  1. At the construction site, provide an updated logbook enumerating the different activities of the project, and post a signboard with the Building/Fencing Permit Number and date of issuance at all times.
  2. When applying for Certificate of Completion / Final Inspection, submit As-Built Plans and the logbook duly signed & sealed by the construction engineer.
  3. ALWAYS BRING THIS CHECKLIST EVERY TIME YOU COME TO VISIT AND FOLLOW UP YOUR PERMIT APPLICATION.
