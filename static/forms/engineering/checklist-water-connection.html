CHECKLIST FOR WATER CONNECTION

Requirements for Primewater Municipal Utility Service Connection
  • Approved Application Form from Primewater: Official pre-approved water utility application form issued by Primewater Tanauan.
  • Tax Declaration R13 (2 Photocopies): Current updated municipal property tax declaration certificate.
  • Mode of Ownership (2 Photocopies): Proof of ownership or legal right over the property (Transfer Certificate of Title, Deed of Sale, or Notarized Lease/Consent).
  • Photographs of the House (2 Copies): Clear photographs showing the exterior of the house/structure.
  • Detailed Engineering Drawings (5 Sets): Complete drawings signed and sealed by a licensed Civil Engineer or Architect.
  • Barangay Certification / Clearance (1 Photocopy): Official clearance certificate issued by the host barangay council.
