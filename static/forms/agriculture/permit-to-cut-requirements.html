Republic of the Philippines • Department of Agriculture

Philippine Coconut Authority (PCA)

Municipal Agriculture Office • Tanauan, Leyte

STATUTORY AUTHORITY & LEGAL BASIS: Pursuant to Republic Act No. 8048 (Coconut Preservation Act of 1995) as amended by Republic Act No. 10593, no coconut tree shall be cut unless a valid Permit to Cut (PTC) is issued by the Philippine Coconut Authority (PCA) through the Coconut Development Officer (CDO) and the Municipal Agriculture Office.
PERMIT TO CUT (PTC) REQUIREMENTS CHECKLIST 11 MANDATORY ITEMS
  • 1. Fully Accomplished PTC Application Form
    Official PCA Application form stating reason for cutting (senile, diseased, storm-damaged, or conversion).
    Required
  • 2. Latest Land Title / Tax Declaration (Photocopy)
    Proof of real property ownership for the land parcel where the coconut trees are located.
    Required
  • 3. Government-Issued ID (*ID Card Photocopy)
    Valid identification of the landowner/applicant (PhilID, Driver's License, Voter's ID, PRC).
    Required
  • 4. Barangay Certification
    Official certification issued by the Punong Barangay certifying ownership and location of trees.
    Required
  • 5. Authorization from the Applicant
    Required if the application is submitted by an authorized family representative.
    Conditional
  • 6. Special Power of Attorney (SPA)
    Notarized SPA required if representative is transacting on behalf of an absentee landowner.
    Conditional
  • 7. Inspection Report (*from CDO)
    On-site verification report conducted by the PCA Coconut Development Officer (CDO) confirming tree condition.
    Required
  • 8. Certificate of Chainsaw Registration (*Photocopy)
    Legally registered chainsaw documentation under DENR/LGU pursuant to RA 9175.
    Required
  • 9. Program of Work/s
    Detailed work plan showing replacement replanting schedule (1:1 replanting ratio pursuant to law).
    Required
  • 10. Notice to Proceed
    Official clearance to commence approved cutting operations following fee settlement.
    Required
  • 11. Birth Certificate (*if son / daughter)
    PSA birth certificate verifying filial relationship if applicant is the legitimate offspring of landowner.
    Conditional
