# Municipal Accounting Office

The **Accounting Office** maintains fiscal responsibility and financial transparency for the Municipality of Tanauan through accurate accounting and prudent financial management.

## Core functions
- Financial stewardship across all municipal operations and transactions
- Comprehensive financial services supporting municipal operations
- Accomplishment reporting demonstrating transparent financial management

The office publishes accomplishment reports and tracks financial-management projects, aligning with the municipality's transparency commitments (see [[Taxes and Fees of Tanauan]] and [[Municipal Government of Tanauan]]).
