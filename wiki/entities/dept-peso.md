# Public Employment Service Office (PESO)

The Public Employment Service Office (PESO) is a multi-employment service facility established pursuant to R.A. No. 8759 (PESO Act of 1999) as amended by R.A. No. 10691 in coordination with the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE).

- **Location:** Ground Floor, Tanauan Town Hall, 888 Real Street, Tanauan, Leyte
- **Head / Manager:** Joselita L. Retaga (Labor & Employment Officer III / PESO Manager)
- **Citizen's Charter Route:** `/citizens-charter/peso`

## Core Services & Programs

### 1. Employment Facilitation & Job Matching
- Provides labor market information, referral, job vacancy posting, and recruitment activity facilitation.
- Fee: Free for jobseekers. Certificate of No Objection for Special Recruitment Authority (SRA/LRA): ₱500–₱1,000.

### 2. Career Development & Training (including SPES)
- Special Program for Employment of Students (SPES) and career counseling.
- **Requirements for Students/Applicants:**
  - Letter of Intent
  - Resume / Bio-data
  - Personal Assessment Form (SPES 02)
  - PSA Birth Certificate photocopy
  - Form 138 (High School / Senior High School) or recent grades / registration form (College)
- **Fee:** Free

### 3. Livelihood & Self-Employment Assistance
- Assisting aspiring entrepreneurs and organized groups with DOLE livelihood grants and startup resources.
- **Requirements:** Letter of Intent / proposal, valid ID photocopy.
