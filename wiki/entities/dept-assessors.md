# Municipal Assessor's Office

The **Municipal Assessor's Office** is responsible for the appraisal and assessment of real properties within Tanauan for taxation purposes. Headed by **Engr. Rochel M. Orit, CE, REA, REB, MPRM** (Municipal Assessor).

## Functions
- Appraisal and assessment of real properties for fair, equitable taxation (per RA 7160)
- Real property identification and accounting system
- Tax mapping system and assessment rolls
- Periodic general revisions of assessments
- Issue updated tax declarations
- Provide technical support to other municipal departments

## Privacy
Operates under the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)**, with transparency, legitimate purpose, proportionality, access control, and data encryption.
