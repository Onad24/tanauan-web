# Municipal Civil Registrar (MCR)

The Office of the Municipal Civil Registrar of Tanauan, Leyte implements civil registration programs pursuant to the Civil Registry Law (Act No. 3753).

- **Location:** Ground Floor, Tanauan Town Hall, Real Street, Tanauan, Leyte
- **Department Head:** Vincent Francis A. Salvaña
- **Staff / Personnel:** Virginia Parmo, Miracelo Vertudes, Ma. Clenia Pilola
- **Citizen's Charter Route:** `/citizens-charter/civil-registrar`

## Services Offered

### 1. Application for Documents (Birth, Marriage, Death Certificates)
- **About:** Registration and issuance of certified copies of birth, marriage, and death events within the prescribed period.
- **Processing Time:** Approximately 34 minutes
- **Fee:** ₱80.00
- **Requirements:**
  - *Birth:* Marriage Contract of Parents, PSA Negative Certificate of the Child, Baptismal Certificate, Affidavit of 2 Disinterested Persons (for delayed registration), Current CTC / Valid ID, Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father (AUSF, if parents are unmarried).
  - *Marriage:* Copy of Certificate of Marriage (COM) for registration; for delayed registration: joint affidavit of applicants duly notarized, PSA negative result, old copy of unregistered document.
  - *Death:* Personal appearance of informant (spouse or nearest kin) if death occurred at home; Death Certificate prepared by hospital if death occurred in hospital.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Proceed to the MCR and verbalize request.
  2. If document is available, proceed to the Municipal Treasury Office (MTO) for payment.
  3. Present Official Receipt (OR) to registry clerk.
  4. Certificate is checked, signed, and issued.

### 2. Application for Marriage License
- **About:** Filing sworn applications for marriage license before contracting marriage.
- **Processing Time:** 10 days and 1 hour (subject to mandatory 10-day legal posting period).
- **Fee:** ₱150.00
- **Requirements:**
  - Pre-marital Counselling Certificate
  - Birth or Baptismal Certificate of contracting parties, Valid ID, CTC
  - Parental Consent (if aged 18–20)
  - Parental Advice (if aged 21–24)
  - Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR) from PSA
  - Duly notarized Affidavit of Cohabitation (for couples cohabiting for 5+ years)
