# Demographics of Tanauan

## Population

- **2020 Census of Population and Housing (1 May 2020):** population of **57,455** across 54 barangays, within a land area of ~78.4 km².
- **2024 official census (1 July 2024):** growth to about **60,212** residents, a modest increase over four years.
- **2015 Census:** 13,518 households with an average size of about **4.1 persons**.
- Source: PSA Eastern Visayas Special Release (May–July 2025).

> Note: The Sanitation/RHU data cites a population of 62,533 and 16,644 households (Q2 2025). Figures vary by source and date.

## Language & religion

Most residents speak **Waray-Waray**, with Filipino and English widely understood (see [[Languages of Tanauan]]). The population is predominantly **Roman Catholic**, with Iglesia ni Cristo, Evangelicals, and Protestants present (see [[Religion in Tanauan]]).

## Livelihood

Major income sources include **agriculture**, **fishing**, and **micro-enterprises**.
