# PhilHealth Office

The **PhilHealth Office** provides localized services for the residents of Tanauan: membership registration, benefit queries, and coordination of national health insurance programs.

## Functions
- Assistance with PhilHealth membership registration and record updating
- Processing and verification of member benefit eligibility and claims queries
- Implementation of national health programs and medical assistance programs locally
- Information campaigns on health care benefits and coverage packages
