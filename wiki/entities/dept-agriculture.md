# Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)

The **Municipal Agriculture Office** supports Tanauan's farmers, fisherfolk, and backyard raisers with free seeds, fertilizer grants, motorized banca supplies, and fair market prices. Located at the Ground Floor, Right Wing, Municipal Hall. **Agri hotline: (053) 322-5401.**

## Crop section
- **RSBSA registration** (Corn & Cassava) — Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture; reliable data for program implementation and policy.
- **Rice section** — seed & fertilizer distribution, crop insurance claims, RSBSA registration of rice farmers, geotagged confirmation of rice areas.
- **Vegetables** — subsidizes high-value-crop farmers to increase production.
- **Farm machinery/equipment** — mechanization of farmlands, helping defray rental fees and boosting yield.

## Livestock section
- Medicines & vaccines to prevent deadly diseases of cattle and domesticated animals
- Livestock dispersal for eligible farmers to improve income and livelihood

## Fisheries section
- RSBSA/NFRS registration and boat registration (master list of fisherfolk)
- Fingerlings dispersal for income and livelihood

## Institutional development
- Assistance registering farmers'/fisherfolk associations
- Livelihood assistance to address poverty

Each barangay has an **assigned agricultural technologist** for on-farm assistance. The office also runs a **Tanauan Indicative Price Watch** for fair market buying prices.
