# General Services Office (GSO)

The **General Services Office (GSO)** manages general services support to all offices of the local government: custody of municipal property, procurement, storage and distribution of supplies, vehicle/fleet management, facility maintenance, janitorial and security services, and coordination with the Commission on Audit (COA). Headed by **Engr. Maria D. Lim** (General Services Officer).

## Services
- **Property management:** inventory, transfer/disposal, acknowledgment receipts, inspection
- **Procurement:** purchase request processing, canvassing/bidding, supplies distribution, annual procurement planning
- **Vehicle & equipment:** dispatching, maintenance, fuel allocation
- **Facility maintenance:** buildings, janitorial, security, grounds/landscaping
