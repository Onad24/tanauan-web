# Human Resources Management Office (HRMO)

The **HRMO** administers, develops, and implements the Municipality of Tanauan's human resource programs, policies, and systems to ensure an efficient, effective, and motivated workforce. Officer-in-charge: **Atty. Federico C. Tizon, EnP** (HRMO III).

## Functions
- Recruitment, selection & placement
- Personnel administration & records management
- Compensation, benefits & welfare administration
- Performance management & evaluation
- Learning and development (training)
- Human resource planning & policy development
- Employee relations & morale development
- Compliance & liaison functions
