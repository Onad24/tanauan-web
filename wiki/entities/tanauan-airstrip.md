# Tanauan Airstrip

A historical **World War II site** built by American forces, the Tanauan Airstrip played a crucial role in the **liberation of Leyte and the Pacific campaign**. Following MacArthur's October 1944 landing, the US Sixth Army set up headquarters in Tanauan and constructed a vital airfield.

Though little remains today, the site stands as a testament to Tanauan's strategic importance in Philippine history during the Allied liberation campaign.
