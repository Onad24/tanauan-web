# Public Market Office

The **Public Market Office** oversees the operation of Tanauan's public markets concerning sanitation, cleanliness, security, and order within the market premises. Supervisor: **Luz M. Maderazo** (Market Supervisor).

## Functions
- Implement and execute Market Board plans and policies for market operations
- Supervise and evaluate subordinates; investigate complaints and recommend action to the Market Board
- Administer market properties (acquisition, maintenance, utilization, disposal)
- Coordinate collection and imposition of fees and charges with the **Municipal Treasurer's Office**
- Recommend creation of necessary market positions to the Mayor
- Render monthly reports to the Market Board
