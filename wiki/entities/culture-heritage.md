# Culture and Heritage of Tanauan

Tanauan, Leyte has **316+ years of history**, 5 heritage sites, 3 traditional dances, and 1 annual festival.

## Heritage sites (as featured on the website)

1. **[[Assumption of Our Lady Parish Church]]** (est. 1704) — Jesuit-built coral stone church, baroque facade, home to Nuestra Señora de la Asuncion.
2. **Balinsasayaw Singers** — a celebrated choral ensemble named after the *balinsasayaw* (swiftlet bird), preserving traditional **Waray folk music**; recorded folk songs like *"Singsing"* and performed at cultural events across the Philippines.
3. **[[Pasaka Festival]]** — a vibrant thanksgiving festival every August in honor of Tanauan's patroness, Our Lady of the Assumption.
4. **Tanauan Public Plaza** — gathering place beneath centuries-old acacia trees, with a mini-amphitheater hosting cultural performances and civic events.
5. **Tanauan Municipal Hall** (est. 1910) — seat of local governance, blending traditional Filipino municipal design with modern infrastructure.
6. **[[Tanauan Airstrip]]** — a historical World War II site built by American forces.
7. **Surge of Hope** — a community resilience project symbolizing unity and shared responsibility.
8. **Hanging Bride (Brgy Balud tradition)** — a rare cultural ritual and folklore tradition preserving ancestral practices in Barangay Balud.

## Related cultural identity

- **Pottery & crafts:** Barangay Canramos is renowned for its potters and quality clay.
- **Balinsasayaw Singers** continue to entertain local and international visitors, with **Danny Basas** as musical director and composer of the Tanauan Hymn.
- **Skimboarding** is a popular coastal water sport — see [[Skimboarding in Tanauan]].
