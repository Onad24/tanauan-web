# Sanitation & Environmental Health (RHU)

The **Sanitation and Environmental Health desk** operates under the **Rural Health Unit (RHU)** and enforces the Sanitation Code of the Philippines (PD 856): sanitation inspections, permits & health certificates for food handlers, water quality audits, sewage and solid waste disposal, and disease control.

## Key figures (Q2 2025, FHSIS)
- Population ~62,533; 16,644 households; 6 barangay health stations
- **Safe water access:** 87.50% of households (14,064 of 16,644); 100% of examined water refilling stations compliant (20 audited)
- **Basic sanitation:** 95% of households (15,657) have flush-toilet–septic access
- **Permit compliance:** 100% of 245 active food businesses sanctioned; 785 (99.6%) food handlers certified; 313 industrial permits (100%); 92 public-place permits (100%)
