# Vice-Mayor & Sangguniang Bayan

The **Office of the Vice-Mayor** presides over the **Sangguniang Bayan (SB)** — the municipal legislative body — which crafts ordinances, resolutions, and legislative measures for the general welfare of the municipality and its **54 barangays**.

## Structure
- Municipal Vice-Mayor (Presiding Officer)
- 10 SB Members: 1st–8th Councilors, ABC President, SK Fed. President
- SB Secretary (custodian of records), Local Legislative Officers, Stenographer, Administrative Aides

## Mandate
A dynamic, transparent, responsive legislative institution fostering progressive local legislation, citizen engagement, and sustainable community empowerment.

## Sessions & public service
- **Regular Session:** every Tuesday, 9:00 AM, SB Session Hall, Municipal Hall Complex (open to the public)
- **Vice-Mayor's constituent hours:** Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM
- Public consultations held for proposed ordinance deliberations; CSO accreditation reviewed
