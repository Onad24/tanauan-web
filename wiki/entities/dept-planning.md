# Municipal Planning & Development Office (MPDO)

The **MPDO** charts Tanauan's growth through the **Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP)**, locational & zoning clearances for businesses and homes, and strategic socio-economic development. Located at the 2nd Floor, Left Wing, Municipal Hall. **Phone (053) 322-6810; email mpdo@tanauan-leyte.gov.ph.**

## Services
- **Locational & zoning clearances** — for business permits and building construction (turnaround at the MPDO window; downloadable official forms)
- **Comprehensive Land Use Plan** — Volume 1 available as a PDF on the website
- **Comprehensive Development Plan (CDP)** — 5 development pillars / Vision 2030 roadmap investing in living standards, civic facilities, and public infrastructure

Office hours Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM.
