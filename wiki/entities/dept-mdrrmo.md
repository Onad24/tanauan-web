# Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction & Management Office (MDRRMO)

The **MDRRMO** runs Tanauan's **24/7 Municipal Operations Center** for disaster preparedness, response, and emergency operations. Current status is typically posted (e.g. CODE WHITE/Normal).

## Emergency numbers
- **MDRRMO Rescue:** 0917-826-2826
- **Ambulance:** (053) 322-4119
- **EOC:** (053) 322-9111
- Fire (BFP) and Police (PNP) numbers are linked on the disaster page

## Preparedness resources
- **Typhoon guide** — plain-language family actions per PAGASA Tropical Cyclone Warning Signal
- **Community safe shelters** — certified storm-resistant evacuation centers with backup power, clean water, and medical stations
- **Family emergency "go-bag"** checklist — one bag per family, kept near the doorway
- Storm/hazard data draws on the **Community Hazard & Preparedness Interactive Map** page
