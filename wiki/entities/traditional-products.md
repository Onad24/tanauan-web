# Traditional Products of Tanauan

Tanauan's local goods span sweet delicacies and handcrafted treasures — "from riverbed clay pottery to sun-dried ocean harvests."

## Signature delicacies

### Binagol (the legendary pasalubong)
Tanauan's **#1 pasalubong** and the "sweet soul of Eastern Visayas." Made from **mountain talyan taro**, slow-simmered coconut milk, and muscovado caramel. Served in an authentic halved coconut shell (*bagol*) and wrapped in banana leaves, with a smooth, velvety taro crust over a pool of golden coconut caramel. Best enjoyed warm with hot sikwate (cacao). An ancestral Visayan recipe.

### Moron
A silky swirl of **chocolate moron** — a native staple, typically enjoyed with sikwate.

### Sigarilyas / other crafts
- **Binagol, moron, and other coconut- & taro-based sweets**
- **Riverbed clay pottery** (Barangay Canramos is renowned for potters; see [[Culture and Heritage of Tanauan]])
- **Sun-dried ocean harvests** (local fishing products)

Products are 100% locally grown and harvested, with generations of traditional craftsmanship.
