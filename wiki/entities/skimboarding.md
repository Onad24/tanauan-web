# Skimboarding in Tanauan

**Tanauan, Leyte** is becoming one of the Philippines' most exciting **skimboarding** destinations. Located on the eastern coast of Leyte facing the **Leyte Gulf**, its beaches offer a strong coastal culture ideal for the sport.

## What is skimboarding?

Skimboarding is a **variation of surfing** that doesn't depend on big waves. Riders "skim" the surface on the **shore break** — riding when the wave crashes and recedes to sea. It combines elements of surfing and skateboarding, requiring **speed, balance, and agility**. The National Skimboarding event was organized "right where it started" in Tanauan.

## Key spot
- **Santo Niño Beach** — the heart of Tanauan skimboarding (with other beaches offering conditions for different skill levels)

## Practical info
- Best during **summer months** when waves are ideal
- Tanauan has produced skilled skimboarders who compete in local and regional events
- Plan your visit from Manila (flight to Tacloban, ~18 km away) or Cebu
