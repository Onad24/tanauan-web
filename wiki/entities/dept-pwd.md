# Persons with Disability Office (PDAO)

The **Persons with Disability Office (PDAO/PWD)** works under the MSWDO to implement national programs, policies, and benefits for persons with disabilities under **RA 7277 (Magna Carta for Disabled Persons)**, fostering inclusion and accessibility.

## Functions
- Processing and issuance of **PWD Identification Cards** for discount and privilege access
- Coordination of medical, educational, and livelihood assistance programs for PWDs
- Monitoring accessibility and advocacy campaigns under the **Accessibility Law (BP 344)**
- Implementation of community-based rehabilitation and skills training programs
