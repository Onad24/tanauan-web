# Geography of Tanauan

## Location

Tanauan is located in the **eastern part of Leyte**, facing the **Leyte Gulf**. It is bordered by **Palo** to the north and **Tolosa** to the south. It is approximately **18 kilometers south of Tacloban City** and accessible via the **Maharlika Highway**.

## Land area & terrain

- **Total land area:** 78.63 km² (the Demographics page cites ~78.4 km²)
- **Terrain:** mostly flat, with some rolling hills in the western part; both coastal and inland zones

## Administrative divisions

Tanauan is divided into **54 barangays** — see [[Barangays of Tanauan]].

## Climate

Tanauan has a **tropical climate (Type II)** with no pronounced dry season and abundant rainfall mostly from November to January. Temperatures range 24–32°C with high humidity; it is prone to monsoons and typhoons. See [[Climate of Tanauan]].
