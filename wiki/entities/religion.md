# Religion in Tanauan

Tanauan is **predominantly Roman Catholic**. Its spiritual life is centered on the [[Assumption of Our Lady Parish Church]], one of the oldest in Leyte, founded in the early 18th century. The church has survived natural disasters and wars through centuries of devotion.

## Annual fiesta

The town celebrates its **annual fiesta every August 15**, the Feast of the Assumption, with religious processions, novenas, and community events (overlapping with the [[Pasaka Festival]]). In **2025**, the parish celebrated its **315th year** anniversary with food drives, inter-barangay masses, and youth catechism workshops.

Other religious communities include **Iglesia ni Cristo, Evangelicals, and Protestants**.
