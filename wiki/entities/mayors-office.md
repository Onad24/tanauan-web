# Mayor's Office

The **Mayor's Office** is the chief executive office of the Municipality of Tanauan, Leyte. The current **Municipal Mayor is Hon. Ma. Gina E. Merilo**.

## Role
The Mayor leads the executive branch of the municipal government, directing municipal departments, implementing ordinances and national law, and guiding municipal development. See [[Municipal Government of Tanauan]] and [[Vision and Mission of Tanauan]] for the municipality's strategic direction.
