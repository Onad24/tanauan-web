# Municipal Government of Tanauan

Tanauan, Leyte is governed by a municipal government under the Local Government Code of 1991 (RA 7160), headed by the elected **Mayor** and **Vice Mayor**, with the **Sangguniang Bayan** as the legislative body and 30+ municipal departments/offices as the executive arm. The municipality comprises **54 barangays** (see [[Barangays of Tanauan]]).

## Structure
- **[[Mayor's Office]]** — the chief executive, currently **Hon. Ma. Gina E. Merilo**.
- **Vice Mayor & Sangguniang Bayan** — presides over the council; 10 SB Members (1st–8th councilors, ABC President, SK Fed. President).
- **Departments directory:** see [[Departments of Tanauan]].

## Key public-facing services
- Business permits & licensing (Business Permit and Licensing Section)
- Taxes & fees ([[Taxes and Fees of Tanauan]], Municipal Treasurer's Office)
- Social welfare (MSWDO), health (MHO/RHU), sanitation, civil registry, and more.

## Transparency
The municipality publishes **bids and awards**, a **full disclosure portal**, and annual accomplishment reports per office (see [[Taxes and Fees of Tanauan]] and department pages).
