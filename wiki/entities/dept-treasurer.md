# Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO)

The **Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO)** manages revenue collection for Tanauan: real property tax, business taxes, and regulatory fees. See the [[Taxes and Fees of Tanauan]] page for rates and deadlines.

## Functions
- Collection of taxes, fees, and other municipal revenues
- Assessment verification and Official Receipt (O.R.) issuance
- Cashiering and disbursement
- Revenue reporting

*See also: [[Taxes and Fees of Tanauan]], [[Departments of Tanauan]].*
