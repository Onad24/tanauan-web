# Taxes and Fees of Tanauan

## Real Property Tax (RPT)
Imposed on owners of land, buildings, and machinery within Tanauan.
- **Standard rate:** 1% of the property's assessed value annually
- **Basic deadline:** March 31 for full annual payment
- **Early payments** qualify for up to 10% discount
- Late payments incur penalties (see the on-page estimator)

## Business Taxes & Licenses
Levied on registered businesses operating in the municipality, computed on gross sales or receipts from the preceding calendar year.
- **Filing period:** January 1–20 annually
- Requires an updated **community tax certificate (Cedula)**
- Regulatory permits (Fire, Sanitary, Zoning) are bundled during assessment
- Business tax gross rate ~1.5% of annual gross receipts (per estimator)

## Regulatory & Administrative Fees
Miscellaneous public charges for clearances, health certs, civil registries, and public services:
- Zoning clearances & building inspection permits
- Municipal health card certificates
- Civil registry (birth, marriage, death certificate processing)

## Payment steps (Municipal Hall)
1. **Assessment** — present tax declarations (RPT) or gross revenue receipts (business) at the Assessor's Office
2. **Verification** — the Municipal Treasurer evaluates the computation, verifying penalties and discounts
3. **Payment & O.R.** — settle via Cashier or accredited banking systems; receive your Official Receipt immediately

*Estimates derive from the Local Revenue Code of Tanauan and exclude administrative clearances and specific fire-department evaluation charges.*
