# Solo Parents Welfare Office

The **Solo Parent Office** (under the MSWDO) administers programs and benefits mandated by the **Solo Parents' Welfare Act (RA 8972** and its expansions), ensuring single parents receive welfare, education, and livelihood support.

## Functions
- Processing and issuance of **Solo Parent Identification Cards**
- Educational scholarships and medical assistance for solo parents and children
- Livelihood training, skills development, and financial assistance programs
- Monitoring compliance with government-mandated leaves and benefits for solo-parent employees
