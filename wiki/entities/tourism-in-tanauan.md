# Tourism in Tanauan

Tanauan, Leyte is a coastal municipality facing the Leyte Gulf that has grown into a tourism and cultural destination. Key attractions and experiences:

- **[[Pasaka Festival]]** — the town's signature thanksgiving festival, every August 1–15
- **[[Assumption of Our Lady Parish Church]]** — a 1704 Jesuit-built coral stone church
- **[[Culture and Heritage of Tanauan]]** — heritage sites, the Balinsasayaw Singers, and traditional dances
- **[[Traditional Products of Tanauan]]** — Binagol, moron, clay pottery, and sun-dried harvests
- **[[Skimboarding in Tanauan]]** — a top Philippine skimboarding destination (Santo Niño Beach)
- **[[Tanauan Airstrip]]** — a World War II history site

Toward its 2032 [[Vision and Mission of Tanauan]], the municipality aims to be a "premier tourism destination, education hub, and investment haven" in Leyte.
