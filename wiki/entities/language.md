# Languages of Tanauan

## Primary language: Waray-Waray

**Waray-Waray** (specifically the **Leyte-Waray** dialect variant) is the primary native language spoken by nearly the entire population of Tanauan. It is the bedrock of everyday social interaction, trade, folk culture, and family life, and is widely spoken at home, in schools, and in community-level administration.

### Bilingual/multilingual literacy (estimates)
- **Waray-Waray (native):** ~98.5%
- **Filipino / Tagalog (bilingual):** ~92.0%
- **English (business & education):** ~85.5%

## Education: MTB-MLE

Tanauan's elementary schools implement the **Mother Tongue-Based Multilingual Education (MTB-MLE)** framework under the Philippine Department of Education. From Kindergarten to Grade 3, **Waray-Waray is the primary medium of instruction**, gradually transitioning to Filipino and English.

## Governance & media

Local announcements, public health campaigns, and community forums are typically conducted in **Waray-Waray** across all 54 barangays. Official municipal documentation, legal ordinances, and business licenses are formulated in **English**, with Filipino often used in ceremonial events.
