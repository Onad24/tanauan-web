# Procurement Office

The **Procurement Office** is responsible for the acquisition of goods, services, and works for the Municipality of Tanauan, ensuring transparency, competitiveness, and efficiency in compliance with **Republic Act 9184** (the Government Procurement Reform Act).

## Functions
- Preparation and consolidation of the **Annual Procurement Plan (APP)**
- Processing of purchase requests and acquisition documents
- Coordination with the **Bids and Awards Committee (BAC)** for public bidding
- Monitoring and evaluation of supplier performance and deliveries

Related public transparency pages: `/bids-and-awards` and the full disclosure portal (see [[Municipal Government of Tanauan]]).
