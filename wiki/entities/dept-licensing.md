# Business Permit & Licensing

The **Business Permit and Licensing Section (BPLO)** facilitates business development in Tanauan with efficient, transparent, and responsive licensing and permit services. Officer-In-Charge: **Rodele E. Maceda** (Business Development Services).

## Core functions
- Comprehensive licensing and permit services for business enterprises
- Regulatory compliance support for entrepreneurs
- Business development & sustainable economic growth initiatives

For fees and filing deadlines, see [[Taxes and Fees of Tanauan]].
