# Barangays of Tanauan

Tanauan, Leyte is divided into **54 barangays**. A **Barangay Registry Directory** at the `/Barangays` page lets visitors search each barangay's history, leadership, contact details, gallery, and portals.

## Named barangays (seed registry)
Ada, Amanluran, Arado, Atipolo, Balud, Bangon, Bantagan, Baras, Binolo, Binongtoan, Bislig, Cabalagnan, Cabonga-an, Cabuynan, Cahumayhumayan, Calogcog, Calsadahay, Camire, Canbalisara, Canramos, Catigbian, Catmon, Cogon, Guindag-an, Guingauan, Hilagpad, Kiling, Lapay, Licod, Linao, Magay, Maghulod, Malaguicay, Maribi, Mohon, Pago, Pasil, Picas, Sacme, Salvador, Solano, Talolora, Tugop.

Many barangays have rich **folklore origin stories** (e.g. Barangay Arado named after the plough "arado," Atipolo after the fruit-bearing Atipolo tree, Camire/Canramos for its potters). See [[Culture and Heritage of Tanauan]] for the town's broader heritage.

> **Note:** contact/leadership details shown on the seed registry are placeholders to be maintained by the LGU. For the authoritative list and current officials, use the live registry at `/Barangays` and the Offices directory.
