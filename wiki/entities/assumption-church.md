# Assumption of Our Lady Parish Church

One of the oldest churches in Leyte, **founded in 1704** (the town's Historic Church era). Built by **Jesuit missionaries with coral stone walls**, it was originally constructed as a **fortress against Moro raids** and later the 1897 tidal wave.

## Features
- **Baroque architecture** with an ornate baroque facade
- Houses the image of **Nuestra Señora de la Asuncion**, one of the oldest Marian images in the province
- Home to precious relics chronicling Tanauan's spiritual journey
- Under restoration as a national cultural treasure under the **National Historical Commission**

The town celebrates its fiesta every **August 15** — see [[Religion in Tanauan]] and [[Pasaka Festival]].
