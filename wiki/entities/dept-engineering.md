# Municipal Engineering Office

The **Municipal Engineering Office** handles infrastructure, public works, and citizen safety for Tanauan: transparent public works, quality building inspections, safe public roads, and fast-track citizen building permits. Located at the 2nd Floor, Tanauan Municipal Building.

**Office leadership: Engr. Rochel M. Orit** (Municipal Engineer; CE, REA, REB, MPRM) — Building Official supervision, infrastructure project quality control, and safety certification of municipal structures. Phone (053) 322-4119; email engineer@tanauan-leyte.gov.ph.

## Permits & clearances
- **Building Permit**
- **Occupancy Permit**
- **Electrical (CFEI)** — Certificate of Final Electrical Inspection

Step-by-step procedures, documentary requirements, and downloadable municipal forms are on the office page (Engineering Citizen's Charter & Forms PDF).

Office hours Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM.
