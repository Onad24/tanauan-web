# MSWDO — Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office

The **MSWDO** delivers social welfare services to every resident, especially children, women, senior citizens, persons with disability, and families in crisis, through prevention, protection, and timely assistance.

## Mandate
Operates under the Local Government Code of 1991 (RA 7160), implementing national laws locally including the **Solo Parents' Welfare Act, Magna Carta for Disabled Persons, Expanded Senior Citizens Act**, and the **Anti-Violence Against Women and Their Children Act** (RA 9262), coordinating with the national DSWD on case referrals that require provincial or national support.

## Office hours
Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM, **no noon break**. Walk in, call, or send a message; a social worker responds within one business day. Located at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion.
