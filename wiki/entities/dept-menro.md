# Municipal Environment & Natural Resources Office (MENRO)

The **MENRO** handles environmental protection and natural-resource management for Tanauan through a citizen's-charter-driven services menu (service processes, requirements, and timelines).

## Programs & projects
- **Reforestation initiatives** — tree planting to restore forest cover and protect watersheds
- **Coastal cleanup campaigns** — regular activities to protect marine ecosystems
- **Climate adaptation projects** — community-based climate-resilience programs
- **Solid waste management** — RA 9003 implementation and waste segregation programs
