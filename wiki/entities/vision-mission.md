# Vision and Mission of Tanauan

## Vision

> "Tanauan by 2032, a 1st class municipality which is a premier tourism destination, education hub, and an investment haven in the province of Leyte — inhabited by a healthy and empowered citizenry living in peaceful and resilient communities; with a competitive, diversified, dynamic, and inclusive economy; a disaster-proof, quality, and adequate social support and infrastructure system; and an ecologically balanced, sustainable, and aesthetic environment — under an effective and efficient participatory and people-centered leadership with a strong political will."

## Mission

> "The Municipal Government of Tanauan shall take the lead in the integration and implementation of programs, projects, and activities; promote strong multi-sectoral level partnerships; and mobilize resources through a strong political will and unity of purpose."

## Core values

- **Integrity** — honest & transparent governance
- **Excellence** — striving for the highest standards
- **Accountability** — responsible public service
- **Teamwork** — collaborative partnerships
- **Service** — people-centered approach
- **Resilience** — disaster-ready communities

## Quality policy

Competent leadership, continuous improvement, citizen satisfaction, and sustainable development.
