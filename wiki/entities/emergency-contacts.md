## Emergency guidelines
When calling emergency services: stay calm, specify your precise location (street, barangay, landmarks), detail the situation (what happened, people affected, injuries, active hazards), and stay on the line until the responder prompts otherwise.

## Administrative concerns
For non-emergency matters, visit the appropriate municipal office during business hours (typically Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM).
