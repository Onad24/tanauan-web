# Municipal Budget Office

The **Municipal Budget Office** drives strategic planning and financial governance for Tanauan: optimizing public resource allocation, reviewing budget conformity, and verifying fund availability across all departments. Headed by **Mr. Ermel Milo A. Pilola** (Municipal Budget Officer).

## Core duties
- Sound fiscal planning and comprehensive budgetary reviews
- Budget consolidation and financial reporting
- Coordination of municipal resource plans

The office also participates in national budget-officer conventions (e.g. the PHILBO Inc. Annual National Convention) and publishes accomplishment reports demonstrating transparency.
