FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE FOR TOP 15 STUDENTS – EVSU TANAUAN
Step 1 — Identification of Qualified Students
The EVSU–Tanauan campus shall identify the students who belong to the Top 15 based on the prescribed ranking/academic criteria.
Step 2 — Announcement of Application
The Municipal Government/PESO shall announce the opening of applications and provide the qualified students with the application schedule, requirements, and guidelines.
Step 3 — Submission of Application and Requirements
Qualified students shall submit their duly accomplished application form together with the required supporting documents, such as:
Certificate/Proof of Ranking or Top 15 status
Certificate of Enrollment
Copy of grades/academic records
Barangay/Municipal residency certification, if required
Valid ID
Other documents prescribed by the Municipal Government
Step 4 — Verification and Evaluation
The designated office shall verify the submitted documents and eligibility of each applicant. Applications with incomplete or invalid requirements may be returned for completion or may be disqualified, subject to the ordinance.
Step 5 — Validation of Top 15 Ranking
The applicant’s Top 15 ranking shall be validated with EVSU–Tanauan. The Municipal Government shall ensure that the student meets the qualifications provided under the ordinance.
Step 6 — Preparation of the List of Qualified Beneficiaries
After evaluation and validation, the implementing office shall prepare the final list of qualified Top 15 student beneficiaries for approval.
Step 7 — Approval of the Beneficiary List
The final list shall be endorsed to the appropriate Municipal authority for approval and processing of the financial assistance.
Step 8 — Processing of Financial Assistance
Once approved, the necessary documents, payroll/disbursement documents, and other accounting requirements shall be prepared for the release of the financial assistance.
Step 9 — Release of Financial Assistance
Qualified beneficiaries shall be informed of the date, time, and venue of the release. Students shall present the required identification and sign the necessary acknowledgment/payroll documents upon receiving the assistance.
Step 10 — Recording and Documentation
The implementing office shall maintain records of the beneficiaries, including their application documents, proof of eligibility, acknowledgment receipts/payroll, and other supporting documents for monitoring and audit purposes.
Simplified Flow
EVSU identifies Top 15 → Announcement of Application → Student submits requirements → Verification/Evaluation → Validation with EVSU → Final List of Qualified Beneficiaries → Approval → Processing → Release of Financial Assistance → Documentation
