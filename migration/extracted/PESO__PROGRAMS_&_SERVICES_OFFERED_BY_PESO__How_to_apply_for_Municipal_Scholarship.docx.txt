MUNICIPAL SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM
STEP 1 – Dissemination of Scholarship Information
The Municipal Scholarship Selection Board disseminates information regarding the Municipal Scholarship Program, including the qualifications, documentary requirements, application period, and qualifying examination. The Board is specifically tasked to provide mechanisms for dissemination of relevant scholarship information.
STEP 2 – Application / Submission of Requirements
Interested applicants submit their application and required documents to the designated office/secretariat.
Based on the ordinance, the applicant must provide proof that he/she:
Is financially challenged;
Has a family annual income not exceeding ₱25,000.00 per year;
Is a graduating Senior High School student or an Out-of-School Youth who graduated from Senior High School but cannot enter college because of poverty;
Has a General Weighted Average (GWA) of at least 85% or equivalent;
Has a Certificate of Good Moral Character;
Has a Barangay Certification proving bona fide residency in Tanauan and no derogatory records; and
Is not a recipient of another scholarship or educational grant.
STEP 3 – Initial Checking of Documents
The submitted requirements are checked to determine whether the application is complete and whether the applicant meets the basic qualifications. The Municipal Scholarship Selection Board is responsible for reviewing and examining the requirements submitted by applicants to determine conformity with the ordinance.
STEP 4 – Administration of Qualifying Examination
Qualified applicants take the Municipal Scholarship Qualifying Examination.
The examination is formulated and administered by the Scholarship Selection Board or its designated sub-committee, with assistance from school heads in the municipality.
Passing rate: 80%
Applicants who obtain a passing rate of 80% are recommended by the examination coordinator to the Municipal Scholarship Selection Board.
Exception: Applicants under the Pugay Tagumpay Program are exempt from the qualifying examination.
STEP 5 – Evaluation and Deliberation
The Municipal Scholarship Selection Board evaluates the applicant based on the prescribed qualifications and submitted documents. The Board determines the applicant's eligibility and fitness for the educational grant.
STEP 6 – Final Selection and Approval
The Board approves qualified applications and determines the number of scholarship grantees for the school year, subject to the availability of funds.
STEP 7 – Issuance of Scholarship Certification
Applicants who are approved as qualified scholars are issued a Certification by the Municipal Scholarship Selection Board confirming their qualification and entitlement to the Municipal Scholarship Program.
STEP 8 – Enrollment / Pursuit of College or Vocational Education
The qualified beneficiary proceeds with his/her tertiary education or vocational course in a college within the territorial jurisdiction of Tanauan, Leyte, consistent with the coverage of the ordinance.
STEP 9 – Signing / Compliance with Beneficiary Conditions
The beneficiary must comply with the conditions of the scholarship, including:
No failing grade in any subject;
Maintain a weighted average of at least 2.0 or its equivalent at the end of every semester;
Submit a duly signed Certificate of Grades every semester;
Attend seminars and meetings called by the Municipal Scholarship Board; and
Report circumstances that may affect the scholarship, such as health problems, transfer of residence, or receipt of another scholarship.
STEP 10 – Release of Scholarship Benefit
Qualified scholars receive a ₱1,000.00 monthly scholarship cash grant, released monthly through the Municipal Treasurer's Office payroll system, subject to government and auditing procedures.
STEP 11 – Monitoring and Continuing Evaluation
The scholar's academic performance and compliance with the scholarship conditions are monitored every semester through the submission of the Certificate of Grades and other required information.
STEP 12 – Continuation or Termination of Scholarship
The scholarship continues as long as the beneficiary complies with the conditions of the ordinance.
It may be terminated for grounds including falsification of records, conduct unbecoming of a responsible student, gross violation of school policies, grave absenteeism, dropping all subjects, or other violations specified in the ordinance.
