ABOUT THE SERVICE: | The Local Government Code of the Philippines authorizes the LGU to impose taxes, fees, and other charges on business entities to generate revenue. Its main objective is to require all business establishments to register and secure a Business Permit and License and pay the required taxes and fees before starting operations. The new business permit shall be renewed beginning the first month of each year.

ABOUT THE SERVICE: | No business entity is set in stone, ranging from simple structures like sole proprietorships to corporations. Some business owners make changes for tax purposes or when acquiring or merging with another company, often due to the need for additional capital to grow the business. The Business Permit and Licensing Section understands the reasons behind a change in ownership and will help the owner determine the best course of action moving forward. Thus, the issuance of a certificate of change of ownership is granted after the corresponding fees are paid at the Municipal Treasurer’s Office. This document is a prerequisite for other registering offices to change the name, close the previous owner’s business, and meet other business tax requirements.
